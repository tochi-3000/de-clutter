Why does Google show an unverified warning?

🔒 Zero Token Custody: Declutter executes 100% inside your personal Google tenant. No external cloud servers or databases ever store or see your emails.

Google shows the "Unverified app" screen for any personal container script created by an independent developer rather than an enterprise paying a $50k corporate audit fee.

To authorize: Click Advanced → Go to Declutter (unsafe) → Allow.
